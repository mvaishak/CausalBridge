# Scientific limitations and next work

- Benchmark directions are scoring references, not intervention-derived truth for every real pair. The 17-pair pilot and 38-pair panel were opened before follow-ups; neither supplies untouched validation for a revised method.
- Pairs and graph groups are the relevant independent units. Repeated prompts, factorial cells, ANM splits and fits are dependent observations. Source-group dependence further limits intervals.
- Track A's checker validates a selected operation. Its planning/schema errors and unsupported outcomes must not be merged with abstentions or correct releases.
- Track B's checker validates binding and typed attribution, not causal truth or unrestricted natural-language reasoning. Model abstentions, binding failures, method abstentions and checker rejections are distinct. V3's three rejections overlap upstream abstentions.
- Thinking and cue effects are conditional on one Qwen3:8b digest, prompts, budgets, seed and opened cohort. Thinking did not generally repair wrong ANM arrows and cannot guarantee misinformation resistance.
- Synthetic ANM mechanisms isolate one assumption mismatch, while real residual-spread checks are approximate and selected after outcomes were known. Exploratory adequacy policies cannot become production gates without new validation.
- The prepared synthetic counterfactual cue study uses constructed cards and generic labels; its 96 planned calls are not v3's 170 completed real-pair calls.

Next: preregister a frozen source-separated assessment cohort, independently check label quality and evidence quality, and evaluate any changed method/checker before claiming improvement. Preserve the current studies as historical evidence.
