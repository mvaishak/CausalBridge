## Gates and status

The roadmap's G0–G4 gates were planning checkpoints, not automatic research-validity stamps. G0 interface preservation, G1 exact witness and usable data, G2 logged ANM, and G3 executable predictor/checker/scorer were substantively implemented. G4 allowed a defensible negative result, which the saved pilot and 38-panel analyses support. The contemplated source-separated held-out confirmation remained incomplete.
