## Recount and provenance notes

The 50 Track A rows were classified by their exclusive case flags; E3/E4 38-pair outcomes were grouped by package, strategy and `outcome_category`. The 17-pair pilot score table was read from its original ZIP, and the thinking/v3 tables from their raw TAR bundles. The ANM policy table was checked against its saved case rows. These are deterministic recounts of **saved outputs**, not new inference or ANM fitting. Original labels, source snapshots, numerical requests and raw responses were left untouched. We did not independently rerun every bootstrap, inspect every raw response body, or redownload the Tübingen source.
