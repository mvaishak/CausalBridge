# CausalBridge research source of truth

**Evidence cut:** consolidated branch `codex/scientific-consolidation-20261122`, inspected through `cc04bea` on 22 November 2026. This is a reconstruction of saved work, not a new model experiment. The [evidence ledger](RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md) identifies primary records and the code that interprets them. Counts below use *cases* as denominators unless a request or repeated split is explicitly named. “Correct” in Track B means agreement with the Tübingen reference direction, not proven causation.

## 1. Executive overview

CausalBridge combined two different research tasks. Track A asked whether an LLM using graph and numerical tools could answer a *specified* causal query with warranted evidence, including abstaining when a DAG and observations leave an effect unidentified. Track B asked how names, observational samples, an additive-noise-method (ANM) card, cautious prompting and a deterministic checker affect *unknown-direction* predictions. The 8 November [detailed roadmap](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf) explicitly separated their oracles and metrics. The earlier [high-level reset](../planning/HighLevelPlan.pdf) says Track B was promoted after the team recognized the narrowness of a known-graph task.

The software built both tracks and preserved unusually detailed requests, method receipts, outputs and evaluator joins. Scientific success was mixed. Track A's exact compatible-model witness is valid, while its 50-case live Qwen evaluation rarely answered answerable questions. In the 17-pair pilot and later 38-pair exploratory run, E4 directional outputs closely tracked the ANM card; ANM itself was often wrong against benchmark labels. The checker detected procedural violations in injected tests, yet its real E4 release policy did not improve correct-answer yield. A later controlled cue study showed that supplying a wrong arrow can move model answers toward it. Those observations support an empirical study of evidence dependence and failure propagation, not a validated causal-direction system or a successful repair. [Track A cases](../../artifacts/final-delivery/analysis/track-a-case-results.json), [integrated 38 cases](../../artifacts/final-delivery/postmerge-track-b/integrated-38-analysis/case-results.json), [v3 raw archive](../../artifacts/diagnostic-v3/cue-investigation-v3-7d4a408-complete-01.tar.gz).

## 2. Original research motivation

The 27 October [Vaishak workstream specification](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-vaishak-design.md) describes the initial known-graph system: parse a causal question, choose a valid graph or numerical operation, execute a trusted tool, and release an answer only when its conclusion matches available evidence. It treats correct fully observed binary DAGs, positivity and explicit assumptions as a bounded setting. The original submitted `DSC261_Proposal.pdf`, cited by the November roadmap, is **not present in this checkout**. Its exact wording, pilot success rule, and original team commitments cannot be authenticated here. The October specification is an early implementation artifact; it cannot replace the proposal.

The 8 November reset identifies the motivation for Track B: supplied graphs make many structural questions easy or artificial. With real pairs, the causal arrow is withheld. The team expected variable meaning and observational measurements to affect LLM choices, and wanted to test whether a numerical diagnostic helped or misled. The roadmap explicitly warns that a graph cannot determine an effect sign without sufficient numerical evidence, that a directional benchmark label is imperfect, and that checker abstention can lower both errors and useful output. [High-level plan](../planning/HighLevelPlan.pdf), [detailed roadmap, pp. 1–3 and 6](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf).

## 3. Original roadmap and intended contributions

The November plan called for a bounded two-track course project, not model training or a new causal discovery algorithm. Track A would supply exact structural and intervention ground truth plus compatible-model insufficiency witnesses. Track B would use one frozen Tübingen snapshot, source-group-aware pair selection, four matched evidence packages, one ANM diagnostic, two prompt strategies, a cached checker, a simple gate and an ANM-only baseline. Gold labels and source metadata were to stay in evaluator records. The planned pilot was 15–20 pairs; larger held-out evaluation depended on eligibility, method stability and protocol freeze. The [roadmap-to-results matrix](ROADMAP_TO_RESULTS_MATRIX.md) tracks every named package G1–G3, N1–N2, D1–D2, V1–V2, R1 and E1.

The roadmap itself was a **revision** on 8 November. It refers to the earlier proposal and Anish extension plan v1.2, neither of which is archived as a primary standalone file in this checkout. Its analytical witness and starter-code checks were plans or demonstrations at that date, not observed LLM outcomes. It also proposed process-error perturbations and source-group bootstrap intervals. [Roadmap, pp. 1, 5–9](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf).

## 4. Original hypotheses and experimental design

**Explicit in the November roadmap:** H1 asks whether names change Track B decisions, using E2 versus E3 on matched observations. H2 asks whether method evidence changes decisions, using E3 versus E4 and an ANM-only baseline. H3 asks whether checking helps, using cached S1 versus S2 on E4 and evaluating process errors, accuracy, yield and coverage together. A separate Track A question asks whether exact graph examples prove evidence insufficiency and whether the LLM handles them correctly. The roadmap does **not** posit one unified success hypothesis. [Roadmap, p. 2 hypothesis table and p. 6 metrics](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf).

**Inferred from the comparison design:** S1's caution could alter abstention and accuracy relative to S0; an ANM direction could be useful if it is reliable and the model uses it appropriately; a simple gate is a necessary comparator for checker selectivity. These are reconstructions, not quoted preregistered claims. Success required improved correct yield or a justified tradeoff with wrong yield and coverage, not higher answered accuracy after discarding most responses. Source-group intervals and genuine unseen labels would be needed for stronger generalization. The later cue and ANM mechanism hypotheses are follow-ups, not original proposal claims.

## 5. System architecture and component responsibilities

Track A passes a runtime case and question through parsing, planning, a graph or numerical tool, and a verifier. The graph utility answers ancestors, descendants, paths and adjustment validity; the numerical engine computes intervention probabilities or effects under its declared CPT/adjustment contracts. A separate benchmark oracle retains hidden truth and witness pairs. The [graph module](../../src/causalbridge/tools/graph.py), [inference module](../../src/causalbridge/tools/inference.py), [witness validator](../../src/causalbridge/benchmark/witness.py), [pipeline](../../src/causalbridge/pipeline.py) and [schema](../../src/causalbridge/schemas.py) implement these boundaries.

Track B selects A/B orientation with recorded seeds, builds E1–E4 runtime packages, saves an independent ANM request before fitting, calls the structured predictor under S0 or S1, and checks cached S1 under S2 without another generation. Evaluator records join reference directions and weights only after the run. The package builder verifies row and method digests before construction. The checker validates case, package, mapping, sample and method bindings and structured claims, and may abstain or reject; it cannot establish causal truth or audit unrestricted prose. [Selection/loader](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/loader.py), [packages](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/evidence.py), [ANM](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/anm.py), [predictor](../../src/causalbridge/llm/predictor.py), [checker](../../src/causalbridge/verifier/checks.py), [runner](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/runner.py), [scorer](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/scoring.py).

For ANM, both directions use a fixed train/evaluation split and RBF kernel ridge. For A→B, held-out residuals are B minus the fitted B(A); HSIC measures dependence between A and these residuals. Repeat for B→A. The margin is `HSIC(B→A) − HSIC(A→B)`; positive values over `τ=0.001` favor A→B, negative values below `−τ` favor B→A, and the middle abstains. Training-only standardization and saved sampling/split receipts are part of the numerical contract. The margin is neither a causal probability nor a test that ANM assumptions hold. [Roadmap, p. 4](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf), [frozen integrated config](../../configs/final-track-b-integrated-38.json), [ANM implementation](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/anm.py).

The original score denominator for each Track B package/strategy is all scheduled cases. Weighted coverage is `Σw·1(released)/Σw`; weighted correct and wrong yields replace the indicator with “released and reference-correct/wrong”; weighted selective accuracy is correct released weight divided by released weight, undefined if none. A model abstention, execution failure, and checker rejection are distinct nonrelease causes. [Roadmap, p. 6](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf), [scorer](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/scoring.py), [outcome accounting](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/outcome_accounting.py).

## 6. Track A design, implementation and experiments

The analytical insufficiency case has DAG `Z→X`, `Z→Y`, `X→Y`, `P(Z=1)=0.5`, and `P(X=1|Z)=0.2/0.8`. Two valid Y CPTs yield the *same* visible `P(X,Y)`, with every cell 0.25, yet effects `P(Y=1|do(X=1))−P(Y=1|do(X=0))` are +0.30 and −0.30. The witness establishes that even the known DAG plus that observational marginal does not identify the effect sign. It is an analytical fact checked by code, separate from model behavior. [Roadmap, p. 2](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf), [witness code](../../src/causalbridge/benchmark/witness.py), [fixtures](../../data/pilot/track_a_10_groups.jsonl).

The benchmark grew to 10 graph groups and 50 live cases with several evidence levels. The final Qwen run made 102 model calls for 50 cases. The [analysis code](../../scripts/final_analysis.py) labels any answerable nonrelease without an execution error `false_abstention`, but this is an **aggregate scorer field**, not proof that the model explicitly abstained. Raw-trace classification is more precise:

| Exclusive Track A outcome | Answerable | Unanswerable | Mechanism in the saved trace |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Correct released answer | 1 | 0 | Verified tool-bound answer |
| Wrong released answer | 1 | 0 | Tool-bound answer to a changed question in `G006_sign_complete` |
| Explicit model abstention | 20 | 19 | Plan `response_action=abstain`, verdict `reason_code=model_abstention` |
| Verifier-withheld wrong operation | 4 | 2 | Plan proposed an answer; verdict `wrong_operation/requested_quantity_mismatch` |
| Planning-stage execution error | 3 | 0 | `structured_output_error`, `failure_stage=plan` |
| **Total** | **29** | **21** | **50 scheduled questions** |

The 20 explicit and 4 verifier-withheld answerable nonreleases are the scorer's 24 `false_abstention` cases. The 19 explicit and 2 verifier-withheld unanswerable nonreleases are its 21 `correct_abstention` cases. Thus the released-answer coverage is 2/50 = 4%, and answered accuracy is 1/2, a denominator too small for a useful accuracy claim. Only 9/50 parsed query types matched the oracle type. In `G001_sign_complete`, the model parsed an effect-direction question as magnitude and planned `compute_intervention`; the verifier withheld the answer before executing a tool. In `G006_sign_complete`, a probability answer of 0.31249 was verified against the model's *changed* query although the user's sign question had a negative oracle answer. This is the semantic-binding failure. [Raw traces](../../artifacts/final-delivery/track-a-live/traces.jsonl.gz), [oracle](../../artifacts/final-delivery/track-a-live/oracle.jsonl), [case table](../../artifacts/final-delivery/analysis/track-a-case-results.json), [pipeline](../../src/causalbridge/pipeline.py).

The benchmark and unit tests establish software capability and exact witnesses. The live run weakens any claim that the integrated model reliably selects and answers the intended operation. The 24 answerable nonreleases without execution error are missed opportunities; four were verifier-withheld rather than explicit abstentions. Cluster bootstrap coverage interval was 0–0.12 over 10 graph groups, and answered accuracy had no useful cluster interval. [Final metrics](../../artifacts/final-delivery/analysis/metrics.json).

## 7. Track B design and evidence conditions

The frozen selection inventory has 108 snapshot entries: 17 pilot pairs in 7 source groups, 4 development pairs, 38 later validation-role pairs in 12 groups, and 49 not scorable under the scalar study rules. Pilot and 38-pair groups do not overlap, but the 38 reference labels had been opened during ANM validation; the later integrated LLM run is **exploratory**, not a fresh held-out result. The inventory reports zero untouched eligible holdout pairs. [Dataset inventory](../../artifacts/final-delivery/postmerge-track-b/dataset-inventory.json), [roster](../../configs/evaluation-roster.json), [data audit code](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/data_audit.py).

E1 exposes sanitized names and units without rows. E2 exposes anonymous A/B rows and summaries. E3 adds names to the same E2 measurements. E4 adds one cached ANM card to E3. S0 is the base structured prompt; S1 is a caution variant using the same evidence; S2 is a deterministic check of saved S1 output. The simple gate tests input/evidence availability, while ANM-only applies the method decision without an LLM. This design permits within-case contrasts, but only for these prompt and package implementations. It cannot establish that an ANM card's apparent authority, numeric margin, caveat, or wording individually caused a behavior change. [Roadmap, pp. 3 and 6](../planning/Detailed_Implementation_Roadmap.pdf), [package code](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/evidence.py), [prompt code](../../src/causalbridge/llm/predictor.py).

## 9. ANM validation and numerical reliability

Early development used small real and synthetic studies, then a frozen 512-row synthetic confirmation and 38-pair real validation. The preserved [ANM archive](../../artifacts/anm-experiments-2026-11-18/run-records.tar.gz), [manifest](../../artifacts/anm-experiments-2026-11-18/manifest.json), [ANM method](../../src/causalbridge/discovery/anm.py) and [frozen validation inputs](../../artifacts/final-delivery/anm-real-validation/inputs.json) are the primary chain. In 32 synthetic confirmation mechanisms at 512 rows, the two saved split seeds produced 26 correct, 0 wrong and 6 abstentions each; this demonstrates recovery in that generated family and configuration. It does not validate real-pair accuracy. At the fixed primary split on 38 real benchmark pairs, ANM gave 13 correct, 13 wrong, 12 abstentions, 26/38 coverage and 13/26 answered accuracy. The weighted correct and wrong yields were .353 and .381. ## 11. ANM failure and mechanism investigations

The saved-data failure audit verified original real and synthetic attempts without new fits, mapped source families and compared fixed splits/settings. It showed the real-data errors were not confined to one source group or eliminated by a single alternate split. It is descriptive and uses opened labels. [Frozen audit config](../../configs/anm-failure-audit.json), [38 case results](../../artifacts/anm-failure-diagnosis-20261121/case-results.json), [metrics](../../artifacts/anm-failure-diagnosis-20261121/metrics.json), [audit code](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/anm_failure_audit.py).

The controlled mechanism study generated 24 base synthetic worlds, each observed under two noise regimes and four train/evaluation size combinations: 192 cells, with paired repeated worlds. At 64/64, independent additive noise yielded 21 correct, 1 wrong, 2 abstentions; heteroscedastic noise yielded 6 correct, 17 wrong, 1 abstention. At 256/256 the respective counts were 24/0/0 and 2/17/5. Oracle-assisted comparisons and residual tests indicate that violating additive noise can produce severe directional failure under this mechanism, while larger samples do not guarantee rescue. These are controlled synthetic mechanisms and cannot identify the cause of a particular real-pair error. [Protocol](../experiments/anm-mechanism-study-v1.md), [case rows](../../artifacts/anm-mechanism-study-20261121/case-results.json), [metrics](../../artifacts/anm-mechanism-study-20261121/metrics.json), [analysis code](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/anm_mechanism_analysis.py).

The real residual-spread investigation reconstructed 262 saved directional residual records and processed 72 paired calibration vectors. On pilot wrong cases, raw IID nominal flags appeared in 6/8, versus 1/6 correct cases; Holm flags were 2/8 versus 0/6. In the 38-pair panel, available reference-direction raw IID tests flagged 6/10 wrong and 5/11 correct, and Holm flags were 2 in each group. This limited separation cannot diagnose failure reliably. Constant-noise controls with deliberately missed quadratic mean structure triggered 22/24 raw flags, then 0/24 after detrending; actual heteroscedastic controls triggered 24/24 both ways. Thus an observed spread pattern can reflect mean-model error. The block permutation is a saved-order heuristic, not a validated dependence correction. [Protocol](../experiments/anm-real-noise-v1.md), [tables](../../artifacts/anm-real-noise-20261121/tables.md), [raw records](../../artifacts/anm-real-noise-20261121/run-records.tar.gz), [analysis code](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/anm_real_noise.py).

The fixed mean-estimator check selected opened pilot pairs 56 and 88 after seeing their patterns and compared them with six correct pilot pairs. All four prespecified estimators retained strong residual-spread signals for 56 and 88 across raw/detrended and IID/block variants, while held-out MSE changes were modest. This weakens a *simple* explanation that the original KRR setting alone produced those two patterns; it cannot prove structural heteroscedasticity or generalize to all wrong pairs. [Protocol](../experiments/anm-mean-robustness-v1.md), [table](../../artifacts/anm-mean-robustness-20261121/tables.md), [records](../../artifacts/anm-mean-robustness-20261121/records.json), [analysis code](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/anm_mean_robustness.py).

An independent ANM decision audit evaluated nominal residual-independence tests on 55 original real cases. At IID `.025` per direction, both directions rejected on 7/13 correct and 5/13 wrong validation cases; a rule “withhold when both reject” therefore removes correct answers too. The frozen policy replay uses saved p-values only, no refit or LLM call. On 38 pairs, the IID both-rejected rule changes 13 correct/13 wrong/12 abstain to 6/8/24, losing seven correct answers to remove five wrong ones. Requiring unique nonrejection yields 6/4/28 and a tempting 60% answered accuracy, with correct yield only 6/38. It is a hypothetical withholding policy on opened data, not a deployed repair. [Decision audit](../../artifacts/anm-decision-audit-20261121/records.json), [policy protocol](../experiments/anm-adequacy-policy-comparison-v1.md), [policy case rows](../../artifacts/anm-adequacy-policy-comparison-20261121/case-results.json), [policy code](../../src/causalbridge/evaluation/anm_adequacy_comparison.py).

## 12. Qwen thinking ablation

That is 272 primary requests, 271 valid final predictions and one truncated S1/off response. Separate activation and preflight calls are excluded. ## 13. Diagnostic-following v1/v2/v3

The observed E4 copying motivated a controlled directional-cue study on the 17 opened pilot pairs. Its five arms hold names, 16 rows, prompt structure and model fixed while varying an *experimental external direction*: none, correct, wrong, correct with an ANM caveat, or wrong with a caveat. Each arm runs thinking off/on. Cue correctness is assigned from evaluator labels but correctness tags are hidden from requests. The cue contains the true or false arrow by design; the study asks about response sensitivity, not recovery of truth from observations. It cannot isolate real ANM credibility, score magnitude or independent held-out accuracy. [V1 protocol](../experiments/diagnostic-investigation-v1.md), [frozen config](../../configs/diagnostic-investigation-v1.json).

V1 software and saved-response diagnosis were prepared, but its live preflight produced two complete schema-permitted outputs with a direction and `abstained: true`; Python rejected the contradictory state. No full v1 study followed. Anish's V2 made `decision` one enum with internally derived abstention state and added fail-fast preflight. It passed scripted smoke and schema parity checks, then its live preflight failed on the first saved call; no full v2 study followed. Vaishak's V3 changed `evidence_basis` from an array to four required booleans, retained the five-arm research design, added a four-cell diagnostic probe, and verified request order before the full gate. ## 16. Cross-experiment results and failure mechanisms

1. **Query/operation binding:** Track A misread the intended query in many cases, including `G001_sign_graph`; exact tools only answer the operation actually called. The 9/50 query-type matches are a descriptive warning, not a standalone parser accuracy estimate. [Track A cases](../../artifacts/final-delivery/analysis/track-a-case-results.json).
2. **Insufficient numerical evidence:** the compatible-model witness proves nonidentification for one specified observational setting, while some live abstentions were correct and 24 were false. [Witness](../../src/causalbridge/benchmark/witness.py), [Track A cases](../../artifacts/final-delivery/analysis/track-a-case-results.json).
3. **Method misdirection:** pilot and 38-pair ANM decisions had substantial benchmark disagreement; E4 answers closely copied those decisions. Pair 35 moved from E3 correct to E4 wrong. This is a case-level dependency, not proof of every internal reasoning step. [Pilot archive](../../artifacts/final-delivery/track-b-pilot/original-real-pilot-01.zip), [38 case rows](../../artifacts/final-delivery/postmerge-track-b/integrated-38-analysis/case-results.json).
4. **Verification boundary:** binding and structured citation checks catch injected errors but cannot test whether a method direction is true. In the 38 E4 run S2 removed three correct answers and no wrong ones. [Control summary](../../artifacts/record-results-2026-11-19/process-controls-summary.json), [38 metrics](../../artifacts/final-delivery/postmerge-track-b/integrated-38-analysis/metrics.json).
5. **Numerical assumption and estimation risk:** controlled heteroscedasticity can reverse ANM choices; real residual spread also reacts to mean-model error, and nominal adequacy flags are not a reliable real-case error classifier. ## 21. Scientific limitations and unresolved questions

- **UNRESOLVED original proposal:** `DSC261_Proposal.pdf` and the separately cited Anish Extension Plan v1.2 are absent. The precise October research questions and approval chronology cannot be quoted from the November revision.
- **Reference validity:** Tübingen directions are benchmark references, not intervention-confirmed causal truths. Source families are clustered; 17 and 38 are small independent-group counts, and opened labels preclude held-out claims for later studies.
- **ANM calibration:** `τ=.001` was exploratory and not calibrated prospectively on an untouched cohort. HSIC p-values and residual spread checks do not certify ANM assumptions. Runtime/NumPy fingerprint limits affect bitwise portability; the audited decisions did not change in the recorded replay.
- **Mechanism attribution:** E4 copying and v3 cue sensitivity do not prove the model's internal causal reasoning or isolate real ANM authority. Explanations and self-reported evidence use are outputs, not direct cognition measurements.
- **Checker scope:** the checker can bind structured citations/receipts. It cannot identify true causal arrows, verify all free-text claims or repair an upstream wrong method. Synthetic corruption rejection does not estimate natural process-error prevalence.
- **Missing confirmation:** no untouched eligible real holdout remains in the frozen roster, no independent model/dataset replication, no deployed accuracy-improving policy, and no completed synthetic counterfactual cue run are recorded. ## 22. Defensible research conclusions

The project implemented a traceable causal-question tool pipeline and a matched real-pair evidence pipeline with saved numerical receipts and post-run evaluation. The exact witness proves one bounded nonidentification claim. The empirical work shows that a statistical card can dominate the Qwen direction output even when the card disagrees with reference labels; richer evidence increases release as well as wrong-answer yield. The deterministic checker catches specific procedural violations, including artificial corruptions, but neither the original pilot nor the 38-pair E4 run shows improved causal accuracy. The controlled v3 cue intervention strengthens the output-dependence finding for the tested prompt and opened cohort. Synthetic ANM mechanisms establish that violating additive-noise assumptions can cause directional failure; the available real-case investigations do not identify a general failure classifier or validated repair. [Witness](../../src/causalbridge/benchmark/witness.py), [pilot archive](../../artifacts/final-delivery/track-b-pilot/original-real-pilot-01.zip), [38 metrics](../../artifacts/final-delivery/postmerge-track-b/integrated-38-analysis/metrics.json), [v3 archive](../../artifacts/diagnostic-v3/cue-investigation-v3-7d4a408-complete-01.tar.gz), [ANM mechanism cases](../../artifacts/anm-mechanism-study-20261121/case-results.json).

Methodological novelty cannot be established from repository evidence alone. The implementation combines established causal graph operations, ANM, structured prompting and verification. An external literature review is required before claiming novelty relative to prior LLM causal reasoning or verification systems. The empirical contribution is the *bounded, auditable comparison and its negative results* in this project setting.

## 23. Proposed research questions, thesis and abstract

**Retrospective framing, proposed after the evidence:** How do structured causal tools and evidence checks affect the correctness and availability of LLM causal answers when the query, numerical diagnostic or evidence itself can be insufficient or wrong?

Focused questions: (1) Can an exact known-graph witness expose unjustified effect claims? (2) How do names and observational rows change directional release and yield? (3) Does an ANM card improve direction judgments when ANM is imperfect? (4) What process errors can a deterministic checker detect, and what does it do to useful answers? (5) Does thinking or a controlled counterfactual cue alter dependence on the supplied direction? (6) Which ANM failure mechanisms and withholding policies survive controlled and real-data checks?

**Thesis:** CausalBridge makes evidence use auditable and some procedural errors rejectable, while its tested LLM and ANM combination does not establish reliable causal-direction improvement. Upstream question binding and numerical validity remain independent requirements.

**Proposed abstract.** CausalBridge studies two limits of language-model causal reasoning. In a known-graph task, structural and numerical tools check whether an answer follows from available evidence. Compatible models have the same observed joint distribution but opposite intervention effects, proving that the evidence cannot determine the effect sign. In a separate unknown-direction task, CausalBridge varies names, observational rows and an additive-noise-method (ANM) diagnostic on matched Tübingen pairs. It compares a base prompt, a cautious prompt and a deterministic checker of cached outputs. The system preserves requests, numerical receipts, evaluator mappings and case-level outcomes. On a 50-case live known-graph benchmark, only two answers were released; 20 answerable cases received explicit model abstentions, four answers were withheld for wrong operations, and three planning errors occurred. On an exploratory 38-pair real panel whose labels had already been opened for ANM validation, the method-only baseline gave 13 correct directions, 13 wrong directions and 12 abstentions. All 26 released E4 directional answers matched this diagnostic. The checker detected injected evidence-binding faults but, on E4, suppressed three correct answers and no wrong ones. A later controlled cue study on 17 opened pairs showed that a wrong external arrow could shift directional outputs, with caution reducing some wrong releases at an abstention cost. Synthetic ANM experiments showed severe errors under heteroscedastic noise; real residual diagnostics did not establish a general explanation or repair. The project contributes an auditable comparison and documented failure boundaries, not a validated causal discovery method.

**Contributions:** exact insufficiency witness; integrated, reproducible evidence/verification architecture; matched real and controlled cue observations; negative accuracy and numerical-reliability findings. **Limitations:** opened and clustered reference labels, one model, narrow prompts, small samples, an exploratory ANM threshold and no independent repair confirmation.

## 24. Future experiments and validation requirements

First, obtain and archive the original proposal to settle historical intent. Then predeclare a new source-separated dataset or benchmark with unseen labels, fixed model/prompt/threshold/checker policies and explicit group-level uncertainty. Separate the manipulation of cue direction from cue format, score, method attribution and warning text. Run the prepared synthetic cue experiment only after its gates, and report synthetic effects without converting them into real-data accuracy claims. For ANM, compare calibrated methods and adequacy gates prospectively on genuinely new mechanisms and real source families; retain correct yield, wrong yield, abstention and execution failure in every denominator.
